# UI Design: Home Screen (`homeScreen.png`)

## General Layout
- **Theme:** Dark mode.
- **Main Sections:** Left Sidebar, Top Navigation Bar, Promotional Banner, Main Content Area.

## Colors
- **Main Background:** Dark Gray/Almost Black (approx `#1C1C1E`).
- **Sidebar Background:** Blends with main background.
- **Card/Section Backgrounds:** Slightly lighter dark gray (approx `#232325`).
- **Primary Blue:** Used in buttons and banners (approx `#0E72ED`).
- **Primary Orange:** Used for the "New Meeting" button (approx `#F26D21`).
- **Text:** White (`#FFFFFF`) for primary, light gray (approx `#A0A0A0`) for secondary.

## Components

### 1. Left Sidebar
- Width: Narrow, icon-focused.
- Items (Top to Bottom): Home (Active, lighter gray background with white icon), ZoomMate, Meetings, Chat, Hub, More (represented by three dots).
- Bottom Item: Settings icon (gear).
- Text below icons: Small, gray, centered.

### 2. Top Navigation Bar
- **Left:** "zoom Workplace" logo.
- **Center:** Search bar. Background is dark gray (`#2D2D2E`), placeholder text "Search (⌘E)" with a magnifying glass icon.
- **Right:** Plus `+` icon, Bell (notifications) icon, Calendar icon, and User Profile picture.

### 3. Promotional Banner
- Located directly below the top nav.
- Background: Dark Blue.
- Content: "PRO TIP" badge (gradient background) followed by promotional text about Zoom Workplace Pro. Close `x` icon on the far right.

### 4. Main Content Area
- **Time/Date Display:** Centered. Time is very large, bold white font (e.g., "1:35 PM"). Date is below it in medium gray font (e.g., "Monday, 7 September").
- **Action Buttons:** A horizontal row of 5 squircle (rounded square) buttons.
    - **New meeting:** Orange background, white camera icon. Has a small dropdown arrow below it.
    - **Join:** Blue background, white `+` icon.
    - **Schedule:** Blue background, white calendar icon with "19".
    - **Share screen:** Blue background, white up-arrow inside a square.
    - **My Notes:** Blue background, white pencil icon.
    - Text labels below each button are gray.
- **Calendar Alert Banner:** Dark blue card (`#1A2B4C` approx) with an info `i` icon. Text: "You haven't connected your calendar yet. Connect now...". Close `x` on right.
- **Upcoming Meetings Card:**
    - Header: Plus `+` icon on left, "Today, Sep 7 ˅" centered.
    - Date selector row: "Today" button with calendar icon, `<` and `>` arrows, and `...` menu on right.
    - Empty State: Centered illustration of a beach chair and umbrella. Text "No meetings scheduled." Below it, a clickable blue link "+ Schedule a meeting".
    - Footer: "Open recordings >" at the very bottom of the card.
