## Overview
This image shows a close-up of the main row of action buttons on the Home Screen. They are squircle (rounded square) shapes.

2.  **Join**

    - **Label:** "Join" in gray text below.

3.  **Schedule**
    - **Background Color:** Solid Blue (`#0E72ED`).

    - **Label:** "Schedule" in gray text below.

4.  **Share screen**
    - **Background Color:** Solid Blue (`#0E72ED`).

5.  **My Notes**

## Hover Effects & Styling
- The image doesn't explicitly show a hover state change (like a color shift), but these buttons typically have a slight brightness increase or shadow elevation on hover.
- All buttons have consistent corner radiuses (squircle shape) and perfectly centered icons.
