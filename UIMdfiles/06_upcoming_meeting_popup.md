# UI Design: Upcoming Meeting Detail Popup (`ClickingUpcomingMeetingPopup.png`)

## General Layout
- Appears as a side panel or modal pinned to the left side of the main window, overlaying the sidebar and part of the main screen.
- **Background:** Dark gray (`#232325`), rounded corners, white border or subtle shadow defining its edges.
- **Header Tools:** Top right of the panel has icons for Edit (pencil), Delete (trash can), More (`...`), and Close (`x`).

## Content Sections
- **Meeting Title:** "Harsh Shukla's Zoom Meeting" (Large, bold white text).
- **Time/Date:** Clock icon. "Monday September 7, 14:15 - 14:45" in gray.
- **Action Buttons:**
    - **Start:** Blue prominent button with a video camera icon.
    - **Chat:** Dark outlined button with a chat bubble icon.
    - **AI Companion ˅:** Dark outlined button with the AI sparkle icon.
    - Below buttons: "View join info" blue link.
- **Join Link:** Location pin icon. A clickable blue URL (e.g., `https://us04web.zoom.us/j/...`). Next to it is a copy/dropdown arrow.
- **Invitees:** People icon. "Invitees" label. Below it, the host's profile picture with a green online status dot, and text "Harsh Shukla (Host)".
- **Attachments:** Paperclip icon. "Add attachments" blue link.
- **Agenda:** Document icon. "Create agenda" blue link with a white "NEW" pill badge.
- **Description:** Menu lines icon. "Description" label. Below it is a horizontal line, followed by the default meeting invitation text (e.g., "Harsh Shukla is inviting you to a scheduled Zoom meeting...", with clickable blue links).
